## Decisions taken for the owner (report these)

The design's decisions were reported with it (spec §20, items 27 to 32). These are the plan's:

1. **The parade is a module beside the movers** (`src/sim/parade.ts`, `World.parade`): a band is one rank, a rectangle 110 wide and 440 tall with a 220 px hole at the floor; its state is wait, rise, march, gone; it is reset by `begin` like the movers.
2. **The catch is a stun with his x tied to the rank** (`stun(h, 2)` then `damage`), so nothing else can move him while he is carried; a slide or a crouch entering the rank passes through the hole.
3. **The drum corps' beat is the sim's own clock** (every 2.5 s, the Basel song's fourth beat at 96), so the shake is deterministic and tested; the knock-off is a hop off the platform with no pip.
4. **The rifles and the charts are two new missile kinds** that fly as the plane does (`flies()`), the rifle on an arc, the chart level; the drill team and the residents are the student's fight with a different throw.
5. **The motorcycles are Paris's loop movers with a jump** (`MoverSpec.jump`): over the ramp the mover's y follows a half sine; the ramp itself is a sloped floor he can walk up.
6. **The errands are a hold on the run** (`Level.errands`, `Run.errands`): 1.5 s still within 60 px of the counter; the door that needs them is `DoorSpec.errands`.
7. **The drum major is a boss module** (`src/sim/major.ts`, kind `"major"`): hittable only while his mace is in the air; the whistle's rank is the parade's band spawned by the module; the followers are the parade's and the game's.
8. **The followers gate the song's lead** (`AudioBus.setLead`): the chanter plays while he moves, the drone holds while he stands; nothing else of the song changes.
9. **The crowd is a trick** (`CastTrick "cheer"`): stands, never solid, never notices, bonkable once with "Prost!".
10. **Missing walls draw flat** (`wallsFor` returns the walls it has and a block for the rest), so the bridge's and the Marktplatz's renders show before the bank and the old town are painted.
11. **The costume is on the level** (`Level.costume`), worn by the look at the level's load (`Look.wear`), the Affliction when absent; the TRAINER badge is drawn by the character for `"scrubs"` only, pinned to the torso below the neck.
12. **Gurneys and IV poles are the tower's movers**; sharps bins are rectangles on the level (`Level.sharps`); the steam is a heat zone with a cycle (`HeatZone.cycle`); the code blue stretch is a multiplier the cast reads (`Level.codeBlue`).
13. **The spray is a trick on the office fight** (`CastTrick "spray"`): a puddle of a new kind at his feet; the slip is the game's (`slipped`: his x speed reversed, `stun` 0.5, no pip, 1.6 s before another).
14. **The replacement is a boss module in three rounds** (`src/sim/replacement.ts`, kind `"replacement"`): the beam is a lit floor stretch the module moves and the game heats; the drone, the IFU and the screen are its state; the residents are called by the game as Titanium's bellhops are (`callResidents`).
15. **The IFU is a box the level owns** (`Level.ifu`, `World.ifu`): picked up by walking into it, thrown by the box key as a box of blades is, re-pickable where it lands, back on the lectern if it leaves the floor.
16. **The screen cracks on the super's burst** (`superStart` within 500 px of the screen); a plain bonk does nothing to it.
17. **Forced recordings are an event** (`voiceLine { id }`): the sound plays the named clip with no roll; used twice, for the curse on his first hurt of the fight and "fired" on the last hit.
18. **The journey is finished when its last stop is done** (`finished(j)`): `finishLevel` drops the snapshot there, `playableNext` returns null, Clock in reads `END_LABEL` ("The epilogue is coming"), `endOfRun` goes home as today.
19. **The home scene is Basel from the suite on** (`homeOf`), him in the Affliction, the parade still, the lanterns on.
20. **Phase 3's cheap leftovers go into Task 1** with two of Phase 2's; the lane-change double count, the undrawn cart, the summoned conductor's walls and the rest of Phase 2's stay deferred to Phase 5.
