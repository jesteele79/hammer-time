**Goal:** The way to Basel plays as the next three stops of the journey: the flight in row 52 as the second 3D level, a run down the aisle against the cart, the turbulence and the purser; Paris with the hotel's bonjour gate, the lift for one, the shut street and the brasserie's French table ending the level; the 14:02 with the station on its own two-minute clock, eighteen coaches and the conductor at three doors; "Basel SBB. 17:06. **Architecture:** 3-1 is the hall's format (the fixed-step sim with the lateral axis, drawn by `ThreeView`) with the cabin as a second set the view loads by the level's name, three snapped lanes in place of free width, forward motion that never stops, a flight module (`src/sim/flight.ts`) for the cart, the turbulence and the rows, and the purser as a boss module beside the others. 3-2 is an ordinary side-view level with four new small mechanics (a wall the lecture opens, a lift that rises while he crouches, a platform that tips, one-way movers for the scooters) and the brasserie's door, which runs one round of the banquet's engine under a third menu and then ends the level. 3-3 is a side-view level whose first stretch runs on its own clock (`Run.stretch`), then eighteen rooms painted at half scale from three coach paintings, the countryside drawn in the windows the cut names, and the conductor as a boss module whose wall stands at three doors. Music is four more `Song`s.

## Decisions taken for the owner (report these)

The design's decisions were reported with it (spec §20, items 23 to 26). These are the plan's:

1. **The cabin is a second set on the one view:** `ThreeView.load(set: SetSpec)` takes the level's set (`Level.hall.set`), the lamps come with the set, and the hall passes its own; the flat fallback draws by the set's name.
2. **The lanes are snapped positions on the hall's own axis** (`Level.hall.lanes`); a tap moves one lane in 0.18 s; the cabin has no free sidestep.
3. **The run is a flag on the level** (`Level.hall.run`): forward is automatic at walking speed, W is the run, S crouches instead of backing, and only the cart's shove moves him back.
4. **The purser and the conductor are boss modules beside the pig, the supervisor and Lifetime Titanium guy** (`src/sim/purser.ts`, `src/sim/conductor.ts`), on the one HUD bar; the purser is drawn in 3D as a cut-out with the tower's cart picture.
5. **The bags are a missile kind** (`"bag"`) that falls straight down a lane; the seatbelt sign is a flag on the flight's state the view and the music read.
6. **The level ends at a seat** (`Level.seat`): reaching it after the boss is down sits him and walks the level off; the flight and the train share the rule.
7. **The bonjour gate is a solid the lecture removes** (`Level.bonjour`); the lecture does everything else it does.
8. **The lift rides only while he crouches on it** (`MoverSpec.ride: "crouch"`), the one way "fits only crouched" can be a rule and not an animation; empty, it comes back down.
9. **The window is a platform that tips** (`LevelPlatform.tip`): after 0.4 s standing on it, the wet-floor sign's slip away from the wall.
10. **The scooters are one-way movers** (`MoverSpec.loop`, `every`, `first`): they appear at one end, ride to the other and vanish.
11. **The shutters' signs, the televisions' flicker and the pigeons are drawn by the game;** no painting carries text or motion.
12. **Paris's walls are placed by an explicit layout** (`BackdropSpec.layout`): the hotel's two floors stacked over one wall width, the street's two walls alternating after it.
13. **The French table is the third `Menu`** and the fourth `QuestId`; the waiter is the server with his own habit (`Hungry.waiter`), no walker; COMPED is shown as OFFERT by the veil; the brasserie's door `ends` the level by the gate's own path to the tally.
14. **The tiny Coke Zero is a food kind of its own** (`tinycoke`, the can's job and picture at 0.7) so the sheet's blank bottle cell is never drawn.
15. **The station's clock is a stretch on the run** (`Level.stretch`, `Run.stretch`): it replaces the level's clock on the HUD while it runs, the level's clock waits, and the card at zero is the venue card with its line swapped.
16. **The coaches are rooms at half scale** (`Level.rooms[i].wall`, `Level.coaches` for the HUD line): three coach paintings serve eighteen coaches; the quiet, family and bike cars are the standard coach dressed by the game.
17. **The countryside is drawn in the window panes the cut names** (`Wall.windows`), scrolling with the camera and a drift of its own; the tunnel is a trigger that blacks them for 3 s.
18. **The conductor is hit only when his punch misses:** a dodged punch staggers him and the stagger is the window; a punch that lands never does. "Faster each round" is a shorter telegraph and a shorter stagger.
19. **The kids are the student's painting at 0.6** (`CastPerson "kid-1"`, `PAINTING_OF`), a new color each, running at him with a shove and no pip.
20. **Phase 2's cheap leftovers go into Task 1;** the door leaf's 0.2 m and his profile's one-frame lag stay deferred.
