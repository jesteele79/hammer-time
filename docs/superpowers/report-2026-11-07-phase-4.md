Nuts drop on the catering table; a full smash (Q) near the screen cracks it, and he staggers out after each crack. The third crack puts the screen dark and leaves him in the open.

- **After that,** Clock in reads "The epilogue is coming" (Phase 5 builds it). Every stop replays from the strip, and the home screen is Basel's street at evening.

## Decisions I made for you in Part C (the replacement)

1. He can only be hit in his openings: in the dark, frozen, staggering out, or in the open after the screen. Otherwise a snot from across the room could win the fight in 20 seconds.
2. He keeps to the far side of the light from you, not its front edge, so you have to cross it or turn it.
3. Bonking the stand throws the light toward whichever end has more room. From the stand's far side, once the light has swung near you, he is left standing close by in the dark.
4. The light shines and heats only in round one. Nothing freezes him in round one, or on his way behind the lectern in round three, so round three needs the screen.
5. The residents take seats in the rows rather than walking in through the doors, with nuts in their pockets (bonk them for the smash).
6. He films from where he stands and hops away only when you come close. A freeze in mid-hop leaves him where he is.
7. The drone hovers beside you at nose height, so a snot fired straight out can reach it.
8. The administrator stops where you can see her, beside you, rather than at the doors.
9. Until his own painting arrives, he wears the student's painting in its purple, so he stands out from the green residents.
10. The training room is drawn by the game until your paintings arrive: warm walls, the calendar with every date crossed out and rewritten in his hand, red lecture seats, the lectern, the ring light, the big screen, and the drone's pad.
11. The music loops a section for each round: a lo-fi content beat, then the bass drive, then the boss drive.
