STYLE (apply to every image):
Inked caricature illustration. Thick clean black outlines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone and one highlight tone, small white specular glints, saturated but natural colors, no gradients, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism, no 3D render look. Same artist and same style as the reference portrait.

RULES: one subject per image; flat bright green background (except the "far" scenes and the title art); no readable text, logos or watermarks; whole subject in frame; largest size; PNG or WebP. Scenes: wide landscape 3:1 or wider, no people, stop at the floor line.
