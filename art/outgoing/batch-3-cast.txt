Same artist and same style as the reference portrait. Use the attached photo for the costume and the kind of person only.

RULES (every image):
- One character per image, whole body in frame with margin, feet included.
- Flat bright green background like a green screen.
- No text, logos or watermarks.
- Largest size available. PNG or WebP.
