POSE AND FRAMING (copy Image 1 exactly, every body):
- Orthographic side view, strict LEFT-facing profile like Image 1: head, torso, hips, legs and both shoes point exactly left. The torso shows its side, not its chest.
- Both arms extend FORWARD (toward image-left) and DOWN, about 30 degrees forward from vertical, elbows nearly straight, hands open, relaxed and EMPTY, exactly like Image 1. A clear triangular green gap between the near forearm and the belly. The far arm parallel and slightly offset so its outline and open hand can be told apart. Never a hand behind the back, on a hip, or crossing the body.
- Both soles flat with the same small forward-back stagger as Image 1 (the far foot a step ahead), toes exactly left, both shoes fully visible.
- The same canvas shape and the same placement in the frame as Image 1: the soles at the same height near the bottom, the figure centered with green margin all round. A taller or shorter person keeps the soles where Image 1's are; only the top of the head sits higher or lower.
- Head in strict left profile, chin level, mouth closed, eyes looking ahead. No hat (the bellhops' hat is a separate item). No props, nothing in the hands.
- No floor, no shadow, no scenery.

STYLE (apply to every image):
Inked caricature illustration. Thick clean black outlines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone and one highlight tone, small white specular glints, saturated but natural colors, no gradients, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism, no 3D render look. Same artist and same style as the reference image.

RULES (every image):
- One subject per image. No collages.
- Background: flat bright green like a green screen (#00FF00). No texture, no gradient, no shadow on the background.
- No text, letters, logos, signatures or watermarks anywhere (name badges and lanyard cards are blank).
- The whole subject inside the frame with margin all round. Feet included.
- Largest size available. PNG or WebP.
- "Profile" means facing LEFT, exactly like Image 1.
