HAMMER TIME — ART BATCH 10: SAN ANTONIO INTERNATIONAL (STORY PHASE 1, THE PROLOGUE)

Eight files for the prologue level, SAT: three walls, one sheet of three standing pieces
for the mid plane, one piece for the front, two new people and one sheet of objects. The game draws
the floors, the rope lines, the X-ray belt, the scanner, the walkway, the bar, the gate
seats and the podium itself (do not paint any of that into the walls). The design is
docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-hammer-time-story-design.md §6.2 and §16.
The gate B7 paintings are a separate batch (batch 9, prompts in batch-r5-codex.txt).

STYLE (apply to every image):
Inked caricature illustration. Thick clean black outlines, flat cel shading with one
shadow tone and one highlight tone, small white specular glints, saturated but natural
colors, no gradients, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism, no 3D render look. Same
artist and same style as the reference images.

RULES (every image):
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures or watermarks anywhere: signs, screens,
  badges, tails, tags and boxes are blank. Southwest's colors (blue, red, yellow) are
  fine; never its name, heart or logo.
- The whole subject inside the frame with margin all round.
- Walls: straight on, every horizontal line level, no side walls or corners, nothing
  standing in front of the wall or on the floor, no people.
- Sheets and people: flat bright green background (#00FF00), no floor, no shadow.
- PNG, the largest size the tool offers; never upscale afterward.

- Background: flat bright green like a green screen (#00FF00). No floor, no shadow, no
