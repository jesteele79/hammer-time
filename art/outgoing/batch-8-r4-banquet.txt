HAMMER TIME — ART BATCH 8: THE BANQUET (R4, HUNGRY HUNGRY HAMMER)
Four paintings for the side quest behind the lobby's BUFFET door: a nurse (the fourth eater), the ballroom, the round
banquet table and the food. The design is docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-r4-hungry-hungry-hammer-design.md §10.

STYLE (apply to every image):
Inked caricature illustration. Thick clean black outlines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone and one highlight tone, small white specular glints, saturated but natural colors, no gradients, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism, no 3D render look. Same artist and same style as the reference images.

RULES (every image):
- One subject per image (the food sheet is the one exception).
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures or watermarks anywhere (badges and labels are blank).
- The whole subject inside the frame with margin all round.
- PNG.

A clear triangular green gap between the near forearm and the belly. The far arm parallel and slightly offset so its outline and open hand can be told apart. Never a hand behind the back, on a hip, or crossing the body.
