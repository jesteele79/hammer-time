HAMMER TIME — ART BATCH 9: GATE B7 (R5, FARE DARTS)
Four paintings for the side quest behind the lobby's AIRPORT SHUTTLE door: the gate (the room), a plane for the window,
a sheet of the gate's pieces, and the gate agent. The design is docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-r5-fare-darts-design.md
§13. The game draws the route map board, the fare tags, the crosshair and everything on the board itself; do not paint
any of that.

STYLE (apply to every image):
Inked caricature illustration. Thick clean black outlines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone and one highlight tone, small white specular glints, saturated but natural colors, no gradients, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism, no 3D render look. Same artist and same style as the reference images.

RULES (every image):
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures or watermarks anywhere: signs, screens, badges, tails and boxes are blank.
- Southwest Airlines' colors are welcome (a bold blue, a warm red, a sunny yellow) but never its name, its heart or any
  other logo.
- The whole subject inside the frame with margin all round.
- PNG.

A clear triangular green gap between the near forearm and the belly. The far arm parallel and slightly offset so its outline and open hand can be told apart. Never a hand behind the back, on a hip, or crossing the body.
- Both soles flat with the same small forward-back stagger as Image 1 (the far foot a step ahead), toes exactly left, both shoes fully visible.
