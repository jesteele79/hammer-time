HAMMER TIME — ART BATCH 11: THE HOTEL AT NIGHT (STORY PHASE 2, ACT 2)

Ten files for Act 2: three new people, the M CLUB lounge's room and its food sheet, the
tower's two walls and its sheet of objects, and (later, once the game has rendered them)
the suite's three walls painted over the game's own renders. The game draws the floors,
the stairs, the rails, the table, the carts in motion, the flashlight, the duster and the
briefcase (do not paint any of that into a wall or into a person's hands). The design is
docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-hammer-time-story-design.md §8 and §16, with the plan
docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-story-phase-2.md.

STYLE (apply to every image):
Inked caricature illustration. Thick clean black outlines, flat cel shading with one
shadow tone and one highlight tone, small white specular glints, saturated but natural
colors, no gradients, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism, no 3D render look. Same
artist and same style as the reference images.

RULES (every image):
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures or watermarks anywhere: signs, screens,
  badges, tags, labels, bottles and boxes are blank. The hotel's colors (a warm cream, a
  deep navy, gold trim) are fine; never a hotel's name or logo.
- The whole subject inside the frame with margin all round.
- Walls: straight on, every horizontal line level, no side walls or corners, nothing
  standing in front of the wall or on the floor, no people.
- Sheets and people: flat bright green background (#00FF00), no floor, no shadow.
- Walls are exactly 1536 x 1024 pixels, landscape, the hotel's shape; never upscale.
- PNG; never upscale or resize afterward.
