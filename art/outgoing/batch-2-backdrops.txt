Attach first: hammer-profile.png (for the style). For each item also attach the venue photo it names.

You are producing wide background scenes for a side-scrolling game, in the same drawn style as the attached portrait. These scenes sit behind the action: show the walls and what hangs on or stands against them, and stop at the floor line. The game draws the floor and everything the character stands on. No people anywhere. No readable text anywhere.

STYLE (apply to every image):
Inked caricature illustration. Thick clean black outlines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone and one highlight tone, small white specular glints, saturated but natural colors, no gradients, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism, no 3D render look. Same artist and same style as the reference image. Use the attached photo for layout and mood only; the result must be an illustration, not a filtered photo.

RULES (every image):
- Wide landscape, 3:1 or wider.
- "far" images: the full wall scene, edge to edge.
- "mid" and "fore" images: only the listed objects, on a flat bright green background like a green screen, with space between objects so they can be cut apart.
- No text, letters, logos or watermarks. No people.
- Largest size available. PNG or WebP.
