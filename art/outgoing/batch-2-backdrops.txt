HAMMER TIME — ART BATCH 2: THE BACK WALLS (rewritten 2026-10-13 for R3)
Attach first, for every item:
  Image 1: art/outgoing/references/hammer-profile.png (the drawing style)
  Image 2: art/incoming/cast/bellhop-1.png (a finished game character: the finish to match)
For each item also attach the venue photo it names, if any (for layout and mood only).

You are painting the back walls of a side-scrolling game, and the things that stand against them, in the same drawn style as Images 1 and 2. The game walks a character along in front of these walls. It draws the floor he walks on, the counters, tables, stairs and doors he uses, and every person, itself. So a wall shows only the wall and what hangs on it, and the floor running away below it.

Each venue has three walls (the gift shop two), in the order the character walks past them. In the game they stand side by side, so they must join: every wall of one venue has the same plain pilaster (a flat column the full height of the wall) cut exactly in half at both side edges, the right half at the left edge and the left half at the right edge, so any two walls of that venue, put side by side, join into one whole pilaster.

STYLE (apply to every image):
Inked caricature illustration. Thick clean black outlines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone and one highlight tone, small white specular glints, saturated but natural colors, no gradients, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism, no 3D render look. Same artist and same style as the reference images. Where an item names a photo, use it for layout and mood only; the result must be an illustration, not a filtered photo.

RULES FOR THE WALLS (every item named "-far-"):
- The widest landscape size your image tool offers (for example 1536 x 1024). PNG.
- Seen straight on, as if the camera stands square to the wall: every horizontal line (ceiling, trim, wainscot, shelf, window sill, the wall's base) stays perfectly level across the whole picture. No perspective running off to the sides, no corners, no side walls.
- The wall's base, where it meets the floor, is one straight level line about three-quarters of the way down the picture (about 768 px down on a 1024 px picture). Below that line the floor runs toward the viewer to the bottom edge: plain, in the venue's floor material, with nothing standing or lying on it.
- The lowest part of the wall, from its base up to about waist height (roughly 100 px above the base line on a 1024 px picture), is plain wall: no furniture, counters, tables, benches, plants, doors or objects stand against it. Lamps, art, windows, shelves and signs hang higher up.
- No doors or doorways anywhere (the game has its own doors, and a painted one would look like a way in). No balconies, ledges or anything that looks like a place to stand.
- The venue's pilaster, cut exactly in half, at both side edges, matching in width, color and light; every horizontal line meets both edges at the same height.
- No people and no animals (stuffed toys are fine where an item asks for them). No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere: signs, labels, menus, flyers and screens are blank.

RULES FOR THE OBJECT SHEETS (every item named "-mid-" or "-fore-"):
- Landscape, the widest size your image tool offers. PNG.
- Only the listed objects, each whole, upright and seen straight from the front, spread across the picture with wide green space between them and around them. Nothing touches another object or the picture's edge.
- Background: flat bright green like a green screen (#00FF00). No floor, no shadow, no texture, no gradient.
- No people, no text, letters, numbers or logos.
