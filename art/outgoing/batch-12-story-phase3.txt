HAMMER TIME — ART BATCH 12: THE WAY TO BASEL (STORY PHASE 3, ACT 3)

Twenty files for the way to Basel: six new people, Paris's four walls with three pieces that
stand in front of them, the brasserie's room and its food sheet, the train's sheet of objects,
and (later, once the game has rendered them) the station's two walls and three coaches
painted over the game's own renders. The game draws the floors, the stairs, the lift's cabin,
the carts and scooters in motion, the mallet, the tray, the ticket punch and the flashlight
(do not paint any of that into a wall or into a person's hands). The design is
docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-hammer-time-story-design.md §9 and §16 with its "As
planned (Phase 3)" notes, and the plan docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-05-story-phase-3.md.

STYLE (apply to every image):
Inked caricature illustration. Thick clean black outlines, flat cel shading with one
shadow tone and one highlight tone, small white specular glints, saturated but natural
colors, no gradients, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism, no 3D render look. Same
artist and same style as the reference images.

RULES (every image):
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures or watermarks anywhere: signs, screens,
  boards, badges, tags, labels, bottles, bills and menus are blank. The game writes every
  word itself ("Fermé", "Voie 23", "Shh").
- The whole subject inside the frame with margin all round.
- Walls: straight on, every horizontal line level, no side walls or corners, no people,
  nothing standing on the floor in front of the wall unless the item says so.
- Sheets, pieces and people: flat bright green background (#00FF00), no floor, no shadow.
- Walls are exactly 1536 x 1024 pixels, landscape; never upscale.
- PNG; never upscale or resize afterward.
