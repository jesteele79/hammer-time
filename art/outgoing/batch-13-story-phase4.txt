HAMMER TIME — ART BATCH 13: BASEL, THE OFFICE AND THE REPLACEMENT (STORY PHASE 4, ACT 3'S END)

Twenty-two files for the end of Act 3: eight new people, Basel's two straight walls and its
sheet of objects, the office's three walls and its sheet, the training room's three walls
and its sheet, and (later, once the game has rendered them) the Mittlere Brücke and the
Marktplatz painted over the game's own renders. The game draws the floors, the ramp's
face, the barriers and bleachers in place, the doors, every sign, the bands marching, the
mace in the air, the ring light's beam, the screen's picture and its cracks, the drone in
flight, the IFU in his hand, the mallet and the badge (do not paint any of that into a
wall or into a person's hands). The design is
docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-hammer-time-story-design.md §10, §11 and §16 with their
"As planned (Phase 4)" notes, and the plan docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-06-story-phase-4.md.

Hammer's scrubs are already painted (art/incoming/hammer/body-scrubs.png): nothing of
Hammer is in this batch.

STYLE (apply to every image):
Inked caricature illustration. Thick clean black outlines, flat cel shading with one
shadow tone and one highlight tone, small white specular glints, saturated but natural
colors, no gradients, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism, no 3D render look. Same
artist and same style as the reference images.

RULES (every image):
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures or watermarks anywhere: signs, screens,
  boards, badges, tags, labels, banners, calendars, bottles and bills are blank. The game
  writes every word itself ("BANK", "SKILLS LAB", "TRAINER").
- The whole subject inside the frame with margin all round.
- Walls: straight on, every horizontal line level, no side walls or corners, no people,
  nothing standing on the floor in front of the wall unless the item says so.
- Sheets, pieces and people: flat bright green background (#00FF00), no floor, no shadow.
- Walls are exactly 1536 x 1024 pixels, landscape; never upscale.
- PNG; never upscale or resize afterward.
