# Hammer Time

Hammer Time is a 2D hotel platformer. Hammer, in his Affliction shirt with a
mallet in his fist, works a shift against the clock: the lobby, the
restaurant, the campus and the gift shop, through bellhops, servers, office
workers, cooks, professors and students who come at him, to the Pink Menace,
the gift shop's giant plush pig. He fights with the mallet, his snot, a lecture
and the super smash, keeps cool with the towel and Coke Zero, and once a shift
can slip through the lobby's BUFFET door into Hungry Hungry Hammer, a
sixty-second fight over a banquet table.

```powershell
npm install
npm run dev
```

On Windows, `./startup.ps1` starts a background preview. On Linux or macOS,
`sh startup.sh` starts the same preview. Both use the app environment wrapper
and preserve the existing Grok platform integrations.

The game is a modern renderer over a fixed-step sim (the design is
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-hammer-time-rebuild-design.md`), rebuilt in
phases R0 to R4 over the first game, which is gone since R4. `/` opens it on
its start screen; `/?next=1` goes straight into play (the checks use it),
`/?quest=hungry` opens Hungry Hungry Hammer's practice, and an old
`/?classic=1` link opens the start screen. It is a PixiJS 8
scene on WebGPU (WebGL 2 when WebGPU is missing, or with `&gl=1`) playing
the shift (the lobby, the restaurant, the campus and the gift shop) under the keyboard (arrows or WASD, Space
or Z to jump, Shift to run, Down to crouch and, at speed, slide, P or Escape
for a break) or the touch pad. `&noart=1` runs the art fallback path;
`&board=1` exposes the look-board renderer; `&dev=1` adds a practice hit on G
and a teleport, a hit and a full smash on `window.__next.dev`, for the checks.

## Voice

Hammer's lines live in one table (`src/game/lines.ts`), each a `Line` with
text and an optional recording. Only the six recordings are spoken, and a
recording is captioned only when it plays; every other Hammer line is a
thought. The cast and the pig speak in bubbles.

`public/absolute-bullshit.mp3` is untouched; its derived `damage-voice.wav`
(outside silence trimmed) decodes on the first key and plays the moment he is
hurt. The five other recordings are taunts, rolled by the audio bus
(`src/game/audio.ts`, `src/game/taunts.ts`): knocking a staffer out, or
freezing staff with a lecture, has a 35% chance of "They need to get fired,"
"They can't take feedback" or "They get all butt hurt" (otherwise, now and
then, a written comeback as a thought); schooling a student, running one off or
swatting a plane a 50% chance of "They got a problem" or "Go grow some man
balls"; a bonk or a snot on the pig a 45% chance of any of the five. Each pool
cycles a shuffled bag before repeating, at least 3.5 seconds apart, never
overlapping or queued. Being hurt cuts a taunt off with "absolute bullshit,"
and muting stops both.

Original M4As are preserved under `public/voice/source/`. Derived mono WAVs trim
only outside silence and normalize peaks for consistent volume. File mapping,
processing details, durations and hashes are in `public/voice/manifest.json`.

## How it is built

- `src/sim` steps the world at 120 Hz over plain objects: the spec's game-feel
  constants, floors with slopes, one-way platforms, solids and passes,
  Hammer's movement, and the rig (`src/sim/rig`: a skeleton whose rest pose is
  the painted body, clips, blending, forward kinematics, face clearance and
  grounding).
- `src/render` draws it: eight planes with parallax, a spring camera with
  look-ahead and trauma shake, the inked venue plate, drawn floors and
  furniture, bloomed lamps, pooled particles, bitmap fonts, and Hammer: the
  owner's painted body as five skinned meshes bent by the skeleton
  (`src/render/skin.ts`), with the head plate and the mallet on top.
- Art: the owner's files stay untouched under `art/reference/` and
  `art/incoming/` (their hashes are recorded in `art/manifest.json`, and the
  sync refuses to run over a changed one). `npm run art:sync` keys and registers
  them and splits each body into skinned layers under `public/art/` (ignored by
  git, regenerated by `dev` and `build`). Everything it stores is published as
  WebP (a tenth of a PNG's bytes, the same to the eye at the size the game draws
  it), Hammer's heads and props and every layer at half the painting's size, and
  the pickups as small copies cut to what is painted. The first frame waits only
  for his body, his neutral face, his mallet and the level he starts in; his
  other faces, the towel, the watch, the stash and the later venues load behind
  it. The painted joints and the layer
  outlines are in `art/cuts/hammer-body.json`; `node scripts/art-grid.mjs <costume>
  [x y w h scale]` draws them over a body with a pixel grid for measuring, and
  `node scripts/art-sync.mjs --overlay` writes each body's layers side by side
  to `artifacts/body-layers-<costume>.png`. `art/ART-REQUEST.md` lists what to
  generate.
- Quality tiers `ultra`, `high`, `medium` and `low` step down one tier at a
  time when a second's frames average more than 15 ms of work or arrive late
  against the display's own rate, never back up; force one with
  `localStorage.setItem("hammer-time-quality", "medium")`.

## Moves

Hammer's moves keep the shipped keys and rules (`src/sim/moves.ts`) on the
new body. When several move keys land at once one move starts, in the order
towel, snot, smash, swing; nothing starts while he crouches or slides, and a
running move is never restarted.

- **H or X, the swing:** a windmill of the mallet that lands in front of him
  (0.42 s). The strike over his head is skipped on screen and still counted
  for contact, so a cart is hit anywhere along the arc in front, once per
  swing. A bonk freezes the action for 0.06 s, shakes the camera, flashes the
  cart white and rolls it away.
- **Q, the smash:** with a full charge (three bags of nuts) he hops, plunges
  and slams the mallet into the floor as he lands; every cart within reach
  flies, and crates hung over the floor within reach open or break. The
  slam freezes for 0.08 s, then runs at quarter speed for 0.25 s, and punches
  the camera in.
- **F, C or K, the snot rocket:** his left hand comes up and presses one
  nostril, he rears back and blows, then flicks the hand off forward and
  drops it. Three charges, one back every 1.5 s, half a second between shots
  and three in flight at most. It leaves just ahead of his nose, knocks a cart
  and splats, or lands as a puddle that dries in 7 s. Hot snot (the pepper's)
  burns orange, sheds embers, is faster and bigger and goes through a cart.
- **T, the towel:** with any heat, on the ground, he stands still, lets the
  mallet hang down in his right hand, brings the towel up in his left and
  wipes his face, flicks the sweat off and shines; heat drains fast. That hand
  and the towel, and the same hand at his nose for the snot rocket, are the
  only things ever drawn over his face.
- **Heat is his stamina:** running (Shift) heats him fast (9 a second, so
  five seconds' running tires him), walking barely cools him (1 a second) and
  standing still cools him (4), and jumps, swings and snot add to it. From 40
  he slows steadily, running or walking, to 0.6 of his speed when it is full,
  and the heat bar pulses red. From 25 he sweats, from 50 he pants, from 70 he
  steams and sweats twice as fast, and near 100 he boils over: then he cannot
  run at all until he cools below 70. The towel (T) or a Coke Zero cools him.

## His other acts, his body and the HUD

- **E, the lecture:** on the ground he plants his feet, points the mallet at
  whoever is in front and wags it (0.8 s), thinking a lecture line. A fifth of
  the way in it lands on everyone in a reach in front of him (R2's staff and
  students), and it cools him 15. One lecture per 1.4 s.
- **Standing still:** at 2.5 s he lets the mallet hang and checks the gold
  watch on his left wrist, from 5 s his near foot taps, and at 7.5 s he
  harrumphs ("Any day now."), then round again from 8 s. He looks bored from the watch
  check on. Any key or move ends it on the spot.
- **Getting hit:** three patience pips. A hit costs one, turns him to face it,
  throws him 540 back and 500 up, shows the hurt face for 0.6 s, plays the
  "absolute bullshit" recording with its caption, drops hot snot and gives him
  1.45 s of grace. The last pip knocks him flat: he goes over backward, lands
  on his back (holding that frame in the air until he touches down), lies a
  beat, sits up and gets up. In the full game that will be a lost life.
- **His body:** he breathes, leans up to 9 degrees into speeding up and back
  when he brakes, squashes on landing and stretches on take-off, and his belly
  follows through (`src/sim/rig/layers.ts`). On a slope his soles lie along
  the floor and his knees take up the difference (`src/sim/rig/feet.ts`).
- **The feel:** he runs at 780 px/s and crosses a 1920 px screen in 2.55 s,
  stops from a run in 0.14 s, and a jump clears 1.16 times his height.
  Landings shake the camera in proportion to the fall and never freeze it;
  falls harder than a jump thud.
Comic words, thoughts and captions are placed off his face every frame, and
particles that reach his face are dropped. Sound is the shipped game's effects
and music through its `AudioBus`, silent until the first key or tap.

## The lobby (1-1)

The lobby (`src/sim/levels/lobby.ts`) runs 16,400 px in three acts:

- **The entrance and the front desk:** a ! crate over the start with the
  first pepper, the long marble desk to jump (its bell rings under the
  mallet), the time clock behind it, two sofas with Coke Zero crates over
  them, a luggage cart, and the BUFFET door.
- **The elevator bank:** an open shaft, "OUT OF ORDER", the only pit. Then two
  routes. Hop onto the landing and climb the 30-degree grand staircase to the
  mezzanine (the M CLUB door is up there), or run on under it past the AIRPORT
  SHUTTLE doors and the STAFF ONLY door to the stash. His jump never reaches
  the mezzanine from the floor. The escalator carries the balcony route back
  down where the two meet.
- **The conference wing:** the second time clock, a second ! crate, banquet
  tables with their chairs, the projector screen, the service corridor to
  slide under, a delivery case and the CLOCK OUT door.

The camera eases out to 0.88 over the mezzanine and never shows past the
level's edges. The staff stash (`src/sim/levels/stash.ts`) has a shelf whose
jump bumps the Buc-ee's case, a ! crate and the cart back to the lobby; it
is stocked once a run: what he takes stays gone, across visits and lost
lives, and its clock never runs. Walking into it cleaned out, he thinks
"Can't keep one shelf stocked." and then says "They need to get fired."

## The restaurant (1-2)

The restaurant (`src/sim/levels/restaurant.ts`) runs 15,800 px in three
acts, and its enemy is heat as much as the staff:

- **The dining room:** the host's podium, the closed PRIVATE DINING door
  ("Opens later"), two booths and a table to hop, the first punch clock, and
  the bar to jump. A busser pushes his bus cart of dirty dishes at Hammer.
- **The kitchen:** the swinging door. When the kitchen server comes through
  it while he is near, she winds up (the ! burst) and the door bangs open
  toward the dining room: standing in its swing costs a pip; a mallet hit on
  the door during her wind-up slams it back on her. Then the pass, where a
  line cook crouches behind the counter with only his toque showing and
  rears up to fling a hot pan; the heat-lamp gantry to slide under; and the
  line, two routes. Up on the steel counters the flat-tops add 20 heat a
  second (a sizzle, steam off his shoes) and the pot rack overhead holds the
  route's best pickups; down on the floor a second cook marches, and the
  walk-in freezer's open door takes 30 heat a second off (frost, his
  breath). The second punch clock is past the line.
- **The loading dock:** a ramp up, produce crates, a delivery case, a busser
  with a trash bin on wheels, the truck well (the one pit: a lost life for
  Hammer, 500 and "DELIVERY REFUSED" for staff knocked in), the truck's bed,
  a server on her break, the dumpster and the DELIVERIES gate out.

## The gift shop (1-4)

The gift shop (`src/sim/levels/giftshop.ts`, "The Pink Menace") runs 4,400 px
on grey carpet with 300 seconds on the clock: a ! crate by the door, then the
arena from 600 to 3900, its punch clock at the way in, two plush shelves (180
and 360 high) and the cash counter (180) to get above the pig, a spotlight in
the middle, and CLOCK OUT at 4100, chained shut until the pig is down.

- **The pig** (`src/sim/pig.ts`) sleeps until he crosses into the arena; then
  "PIG FIGHT", he gets the pepper (hot, full patience) and 1.2 s of grace, and
  he is held between the arena's edges. Its pattern is the shipped one at the
  new scale: a wind-up (1.1 s, then 0.9, 0.7 at half its HP) facing him, or
  away from an arena end; a charge (880 px/s, 1040 at half HP) or, every third
  attack, a hop ("BOING!" first); then a 1.35 s rest, the window.
- **12 HP**: the mallet 1 once a swing, a snot 1 hot or not (it stops every
  snot), the super smash 2 as a bonk, 0.38 s between hits that count. Short of
  3 bonks or 3 snots it holds at 1 HP with MORE BONKS or MORE SNOT; down, it is
  3000 points, "STUFFING SETTLED", time slows, and the chain falls off the exit.
- Landing on it bounces him off; walking into it costs a pip outside grace; the
  lecture only makes him think "It's a stuffed animal." While he is not hot and
  no pepper is out, one appears just inside the arena's left edge every 3 s; the
  pig's charges never reach that corner. A lost life restarts the fight.
- The HUD shows the pig's twelve-segment bar with its bonks and snots, and the
  hint names the rule; the camera frames both fighters. `src/sim/drive.ts`
  fights it from the corner and wins with three lives intact (in
  `pig.check.mjs`).

**The pig's painting** (`art/incoming/pig/`) is cut by `art/cuts/pig.json`
into seven layers on a skeleton of its own (far arm, far leg and tail behind
the body, then the near leg, the head with its ears, and the near arm) and bent
like the cast (`src/sim/rig/pig.ts`: breathing, the wind-up's lean back, the
charge's lean forward with its legs cycling, the hop's stretch and landing
squash, the deflation). It wears the squint, charge, hurt and defeated face
edits by state, flashes white on a hit and throws one of the owner's stuffing
tufts. Without its painting, or with `&noart=1`, the code-drawn pig stands in.
`node scripts/art-grid.mjs pig` draws its cut over the painting.

## Hungry Hungry Hammer (the BUFFET door)

The lobby's BUFFET door (4600) opens Hungry Hungry Hammer once a shift: ↓ on the floor in front of it, as at the STAFF
ONLY door (not in the air, mid-move or knocked down), shows its card ("Side quest / Hungry Hungry Hammer / The
Banquet") and then a sixty-second round at a banquet table. The lobby waits behind it: nothing there moves, its clock,
his heat, the shield and the pepper hold. The tally's Continue (Enter, Space or "Back to the lobby") stands him at the
door with 0.6 s of grace (the key that closed it neither jumps nor swings), and he brings home the round's score, a can
for the Coke Zero, a full smash for the nuts, the Buc-ee's shield as he walks out, and the pepper with full patience for
the hot sauce or a full belly (GOOD TIPPER or better); no lives. **COMPED wins a doggy bag and the belly-flop** (see
below): the round's card names the prizes, the score turns gold once they are his, and the hint on the way back says
what the banquet paid ("Banquet +31,900 · doggy bag packed · belly-flop"). The door then hangs a THANK YOU card and stays shut
through the stash and lost lives; a new shift opens it again. The restaurant's PRIVATE DINING stays closed for R8.

- **The table** (`src/sim/quests/hungry/table.ts`): round, seen from above at an angle as the owner painted it, its top
  a 1500-wide oval 0.37 as deep as wide with its cloth 370 px to the floor; everyone at the banquet is drawn at twice
  their shift size, so the tabletop reaches their belly. Hammer stands at the left end in his banquet bib, the student
  and the bellhop behind the far rim (the table hides them below the waist), the nurse at the right end; the server
  walks behind the far side to his corner and back. The rules work on the top as a circle of 750.
- **The dive**: tap Space, F or ↑ and he belly-flops out along his line, his hand 520 px out in 0.14 s, a 0.04 s hold,
  back in 0.10 s; whatever its 110 px sweep crosses on the way out rides in his hand and flies home to his plate in
  0.25 s, scoring as it lands. One dive every 0.3 s; holding the key never repeats one. His reach grows 10 px a dish
  eaten, to 760. Held past 0.12 s the press charges, to 1180 at 0.5 s (a marker shows where it stops); a charged dive
  that ends on a rival's plate robs its top dish ("YOINK!", its value plus 100). His body tips 40 degrees over the
  cloth while a head lift keeps his face level (`src/sim/rig/dive.ts`).
- **Steering** (2026-10-18): a tap of ← or → hops his line onto the next diner's plate that way (← toward the far side,
  → toward the near side) and locks it there, ringed in amber; holding Space then charges straight at that plate and
  goes out the moment its reach gets there, stopping on the plate and robbing its top dish (a tap whose reach covers a
  locked plate robs it too). Held, ← and → sweep his line freely up to 30 degrees either way (after a hop, once 0.2 s
  have passed), and it stays where it is left. His line is always drawn, in amber over a dark edge so it reads on the
  white cloth, out to where his hand would stop, and the dishes it would sweep are ringed.
- **The rivals** (hard since 2026-10-18) react to food coming to rest within 450 px of their plates (the student
  0.45 s, the bellhop 0.33, the nurse 0.24), mash bursts of 2 to 5 dives, and grow a fifth quicker for each plate he
  robs or lecture he gives (to four levels, calming one every 12 s). Robbed, the student throws a paper plane ("Heads
  up!": 150 points off, unless he ducks, swats it or is out on the table) and the bellhop slides a suitcase onto his line
  for 4 s ("Your bag, sir."). Two hands out within 110 px clash: both come back empty and the food near them scatters.
  The server clears food resting at the rim beside her.
- **They guard their plates**: his hand within 300 px of a diner's plate starts their slap, which comes after their
  reaction times 0.6 (calm: the student 0.27 s, the bellhop 0.20, the nurse 0.14; never under 0.1); a hand still there
  is slapped ("SLAP!" and their line; on every third slap of the round Hammer says it: the "absolute bullshit"
  recording with its caption).
  What his hand held drops on the cloth, what it had sent home is knocked out of the air, 100 points off and a 1.2 s
  stun; their hand lunges at his. A clean rob of a calm student is safe; the bellhop's line runs through the student's
  guard and the nurse is too quick, so the far plates want a lecture first. A guard shows red where his line runs into
  it, and bright while a slap is coming.
- **E lectures the table** (2026-10-18): his thought is a food or table-manners insult ("Elbows OFF the table.", "I've
  had better brisket at a gas station.") or, with the server at his end, one about the service ("Refill. My. Tea.").
  A fifth of the way into its 0.8 s every diner and the server freeze for 2.2 s (no dives, no slaps, no clearing), each
  answering in a bubble ("Okay, boomer.", "Very good, sir.", "Sir, chew your food.", "I'll get my manager."), and come
  out of it a level angrier. Once every 7 s; no dive meanwhile.
- **H bonks** the rim in front of him: food within 300 px jumps toward the middle, a rival's hand there is stunned 2
  s, the server at his corner drops her tray (four dishes) and leaves for 15 s, a suitcase is slammed off, paper is
  swatted. ↓ ducks under the edge. P or Escape is the break.
- **The courses**: 24, 12 and 12 appetizers poured from the chute at 0, 10 and 20 s; 16 and 10 entrees at 25 and 35 s;
  the dessert cart's crash at 42 s spilling 14 desserts; 10 more at 50 s; the last 8 s a frenzy, three dishes a
  second; the six specials once each between 5 and 50 s (Coke Zero, the nuts and Buc-ee's are the products' own
  pictures; the hot sauce, the to-go box and the check come from the food sheet).
- **The score**: the shipped values; grabs less than 1.2 s apart double from the fifth and triple from the tenth (the
  bonus counted apart); the to-go box doubles the next five; the check costs 400, a paper hit 150 and a slap 100. The
  grade lines keep the shipped 3000, 2000 and 1200 in proportion, scaled by the scripted rounds
  (`node --loader ./scripts/game-test-loader.mjs scripts/banquet-tune.mjs`: over ten seeds standing still scores 0,

  by 10.1: COMPED from 30300, GOOD TIPPER from 20200, SPLIT THE CHECK from 12120, else WATER ONLY. The tally names the
  grade, the score and the best, the doggy bag and the belly-flop won or what wins them, and what the round came to
  (slaps among it).
- **Practice**: the start screen's Side quests card plays a round on the same page, and `/?quest=hungry` opens one
  straight away; its tally offers Play again and Start screen, and never touches a shift. Door rounds and practice
  share one best (`hammer-time-best-banquet`).
- **The screen**: the top bar shows the round's score (gold past COMPED) and its clock; the panel reads "Hungry Hungry
  Hammer / The Banquet" with his reach (the charge growing past a tick at his tap's), his combo and the lecture's wait
  (not on a phone on its side, where the pad's lit Lecture says it); the hint names what matters most; the break card
  lists the banquet's keys; the touch pad's arrows hop and aim, Jump dives (hold to charge), Lecture lectures, Hammer
  bonks and ↓ ducks. The camera frames the whole table, all four eaters and the chute between the bars on a laptop and on a
  phone either way; the cards sit on the right half, away from his face, on a wide screen.

**The doggy bag** (owner, 2026-10-18: make the side quest mean something). COMPED from the BUFFET door sends him back
to the lobby with a doggy bag, shown as a takeout box beside his patience. It rides with him through the shift's venues
and a lost life, until the hit that would knock him flat: he eats his leftovers instead ("LEFTOVERS!", his thought
"Saved it for later."), is only hurt, and is back at full patience with the pepper. One bag; practice wins none (its
tally says what the door would have given).

**The belly-flop** (owner, 2026-10-18, the other reward). The same win teaches him the banquet's belly-flop for the rest
of the shift, through its venues and lost lives (a new shift starts without it; practice teaches nothing). His slide
(run, then ↓) goes in on his belly: the same speed, length and 200 px of height, so it still passes under the service
corridor and the heat-lamp gantry, but anyone on their feet it runs into, from his collider to the mallet's head 300 px
ahead, is knocked over ("FLOP!", `strikeNpc` by `flop`), a rush coming at him included, and scored as a runaway cart's
chain (100, 200, 400 and on). The panel shows it as a gold flopping figure beside the doggy bag, the tally says it was
learned, and until his first flop the hint teaches it ("Belly-flop · run, then ↓ · it knocks staff over"). Drawn, it is
the banquet's dive taken all the way to the floor (`flopPose` in `src/sim/rig/dive.ts`, the `flop` clip): he goes over in
0.14 s and lies flat on his belly, his legs trailing with the feet kicked up, both arms down to the floor ahead with the
mallet lying out in front, his neck up and his head level, a 15-degree arch keeping his face clear of his shoulders; the
`belly` grounding rests his belly on the floor. The rig checks hold his face, head and floor in every frame and every
blend in and out, and the `flop` look board draws it frame by frame under the corridor's ceiling. No door opens on the
way past it in a slide or a flop.

The banquet is a mode of the Game (`questCard`, `quest`, `questTally`) over its own sim (`src/sim/quests/hungry/`:
`food.ts` the dishes' disc physics, `hungry.ts` the round, `drive.ts` the scripted players) and drawn by
`src/render/quests/` (`hungry-scene.ts` the scene, `diner.ts` the rivals and the server on their paintings,
`frame.ts` the camera's frame). **The paintings**: the ballroom (`art/cuts/ballroom.json` places the table's middle on
it and sets its scale), the table (`art/cuts/banquet-table.json`, its top's oval on the gold rim), the food sheet (cut
at sync into its sixteen dishes by the green between them) and the nurse (`art/cuts/cast-nurse.json`, five layers and
her head like the cast); `node scripts/art-grid.mjs banquet/room`, `banquet/table` and `cast/nurse` draw their cuts.
The chute, the dessert cart, the suitcase, the paper, the plates, the tray and the THANK YOU card are drawn in code.
Without a painting (or with `&noart=1`) a warm wall and drawn carpet, a drawn table, plates with three-letter tags and
the drawn stand-ins take their places.

## The walls

Every venue of the shift stands in front of the owner's painted walls (the
lobby's three, the restaurant's three, the campus's three and the gift shop's
two, in walking order, cropped at their pilasters by
`art/cuts/wall-<name>.json`) with painted pieces standing against them and in
front of the play, cut from the owner's sheets at sync; the walls are scaled to
span the venue and rise with the ground he stands on. A venue whose walls are
not all in keeps its inked photo (the lobby its drawn columns and palms too).
`node scripts/art-grid.mjs wall/<name>` draws a wall with its seams and floor
line.

the job:

- **Two bellhops** (the desk, the floor route under the balcony), each gripping
  a luggage cart. He squares up with a ! burst, the wheels squeal and he shoves
  the cart at Hammer: jump it (the cases are what hurts), or bonk it back,
  which knocks it out of his hands and bowls him over with it. He follows the
  cart where it stops and wheels it home. Three hits put a bellhop out; the
  last, landing while he is down, sends him sliding into whoever is next.
- **Three servers** (the BUFFET door, the STAFF ONLY door, between the banquet
  tables) carrying trays of water. A warning crouch, then a two-second rush;
  the mallet meets her and the glasses shatter into a puddle of water.
- **Two office workers.** One marches the elevator bank on his phone (it chimes
  as he closes) and knocks over whoever meets him head-on; walked into from
  behind, he only staggers. The other hides behind
  the projector screen, his silhouette on the sheet, and leaps out when Hammer
  is near, flinging his coffee: a puddle that staff slip on.
- **Four students** (three at the mezzanine rail, one sitting on the far
  table). They keep out of mallet reach, hopping back, and throw paper planes
  at his chest every two and a half to four seconds, four in the air at most;
  they duck the mallet, hop over a stomp, hop back from a first snot and run
  from the second.

Since 2026-10-15 they are tougher and pushier (the owner's call): a server, an
office worker or a student takes two hits to put out and a bellhop three; short
of the last they get back up and come at him again, and they can be hit again
where they lie. Up to three of them attack at once; the rest hold their ground
until a slot opens. Whatever hurts him (a cart's cases, a rush, a shoulder, a
plane, the coffee) costs a pip, and nothing touches him in grace or down. The
mallet and the stomp knock staff down, the lecture freezes staff for a
breath and schools students for nine seconds, a puddle trips staff, a big hit
(the smash, the shield, a thrown body) sends them flying, and a flying body
bowls whoever it lands on: the chain pays 100, 200, 400, 800, 1000, 2000, 4000
and 8000, the eighth a life. Points otherwise: a knockdown 100, a slip 150, a
schooling 150, a freeze 50, a swatted plane 50, a shield bowl 200, a student
run off 100, a fall down the shaft 500. Hammer's six recordings answer the cast
(see Voice). `/?next=1&nocast=1` plays the lobby without
them (the older checks use it). `scripts/cast-browser.check.mjs` plays the
fights in the browser and the boards `cast.png`, `cast-fx.png` and
`cast-lobby-1..6.png` show every person in every act, the effects and the
six scenes.

**The cast is painted** like Hammer (R2b-2). Each of the eight people is the
owner's painting under `art/incoming/cast/`, made with Codex from
`art/outgoing/batch-3-codex.txt` and pose-locked to Hammer's body plate, cut by
`art/cuts/cast-<person>.json` (his five layers plus a head) and synced by
`castRig` (`scripts/art-cut.mjs`) into skinned layers, a head plate,
silhouettes and a rig under `public/art/cast/`. In the game
(`src/render/cast-body.ts`, `PaintedFigure`) each person bends on their own
skeleton (`skeletonFrom`: their bone lengths, their arms turned to Hammer's
stance so every pose and reach lands the same way); the head grows to the
share of the height the owner picked (0.22, `LOOKS[...].head`) above a
jaw-to-nape line while the painted neck keeps fitting its collar
(`taperHead`). The bellhops wear the owner's hat (worn without its strap's
loop, whole when it flies off). The three repeats are recolored at sync from
garment masks in their painting's cut (a burgundy vest, a mustard overshirt, a
teal hoodie) and drawn a few percent taller or shorter. The side facing the
nearest lamp gets a thin warm edge (off at the low quality tier). A face edit,
`<person>-silly.png`, swaps in while dazed when the owner makes one. Anyone
whose painting is missing or fails to load draws as the code-drawn stand-in,
and `&noart=1` draws them all that way (`window.__next.castArt` says who is
which). To check a new painting, `node scripts/art-pose.mjs <person>` lays it
over Hammer's; `node scripts/art-grid.mjs cast/<person>` draws its cut over
it; `node scripts/art-sync.mjs --overlay` writes its layers side by side; the
board `cast-heads.png` shows each head size beside Hammer, and
`scripts/cast-art-browser.check.mjs` checks the paintings load and fall back.

## The shift

- **A shift** runs the venues in order: the lobby (1-1), the restaurant
  (1-2), the campus (1-3) and the gift shop with the pig (1-4). It
  starts on the lobby's card with three lives, two patience pips and 420
  seconds. Each venue has its own clock (420 seconds), its own punch clocks
  and its own seeded pickups and crates; under 80 seconds the clock turns
  red and the music hurries.
- **Between venues:** walking into a venue's exit counts the leftover clock
  into 50 points a second, then the next venue's card ("Your shift goes
  on"). Score, lives, cans, the smash charge, patience pips and the pepper
  carry on; he arrives cool with full snot, and a Buc-ee's shield ends at
  the exit. After the gift shop the card is "Shift over", with the score
  and the best. `/?next=1&venue=restaurant`, `&venue=campus` and
  `&venue=arena` start a fresh shift there straight into play, for the
  checks.
- **Pickups** are laid out fresh each run by the shipped seeded rule (anchors
  on floors and platforms, Buc-ee's and nuts first, the rest cans), and each
  route always gets a can, nuts and a Buc-ee's. They are the real products
  (the shipped pictures in `public/`: Coca-Cola Zero Sugar, Mixed Nuts and
  Buc-ee's Beaver Nuggets), about a quarter of his height, and each has one
  job, which the hint line names for two seconds after he takes it:
  - **Coke Zero cools him down:** 25 heat off with a frosty fizz, so he runs
    at full speed again; 200 points, and every hundred cans is a life.
  - **Nuts are smash fuel:** three bags fill the Super Smash; 500 points.
  - **Buc-ee's is a shield:** ten seconds of a gold glow behind him (it
    flickers through its last two), nothing hurts him, and whatever he runs
    into goes flying; 1000 points. The HUD counts it down beside the beaver.
  - **The pepper is a hot head:** an extra hit point and hot snot; 1000 points.
- **Crates** open to a head bump from below or the mallet: the ! crate lets
  out a pepper (nuts if he is already hot) that rises, slides off and turns at
  walls; the Coke Zero crate pops a can; a delivery case pays a can a hit and
  breaks after five, under the mallet or the smash, or at once when he has two
  pips. Carts still roll when bonked but add no smash charge.
- **Punch clocks** save where he comes back: walking past one punches its
  BREAK card gold and the HUD says "Checkpoint saved".
- **Losing a life:** the last pip's knockdown once he lies still, a fall down
  the shaft or the truck well, or the clock running out. He comes back at the
  last punch clock in the venue he is in, with two pips, the full clock and
  the venue's pickups and crates as the shift laid them out. At no lives it
  is "Hammer down", and Try again starts a new shift where the last one
  started.
- **Doors:** Down in front of the staff door goes to the stash and Down at its
  cart comes back, with the lobby just as he left it. The BUFFET, AIRPORT
  SHUTTLE and M CLUB doors carry cards saying what opens them. Walking into
  CLOCK OUT ends the lobby and the shift goes on in the restaurant.
- **The shell:** the HUD's top bar carries the score, the Coke Zero count,
  lives as small portraits, the Buc-ee's boost, the clock, and pause and mute
  buttons; the panel keeps patience, heat, snot and smash; the bottom line is
  the shipped game's contextual hint. The cards (the venue card, "On break"
  with the keys, "Hammer down", "Shift over" with the score and the best,
  kept in this browser) sit in the lower half, clear of his face. On phones
  and touch screens a ten-button pad runs along the bottom.

## The start screen

At `/` (or `/?next=1&title=1`) the start
screen (`src/components/next-start.tsx`) shows the title over the key art,
where to start (the whole shift from the lobby, the restaurant, the campus, or
pig practice at the gift shop), Clock in, the personal best, sound, the keys,
the quality (Auto steps down by itself; High, Medium and Low are kept on the
device) and a Side quests row whose Hungry Hungry Hammer card (its picture drawn
by the game) plays the banquet as practice on the same page. The game loads
behind it, so clocking in is quick; a start chosen before it is ready begins as
soon as it is. It opens as a title sequence, once a page: the lobby comes up
between letterbox bars and the camera creeps in, HAMMER rises, TIME. drops and
lands with a jolt and a brass flash, and the four starts arrive along the bottom
like a filmstrip. Clocking in plunges the camera at Hammer as the bars shut;
back from a shift the screen only fades in, and with reduced motion it stands
finished and clocks in at once. A later start begins like a fresh shift there. Pig practice ends
on "Practice over" and keeps no best. "Start screen" beside Play again and Try
again comes back to it. Its pictures are rendered by the game:
`BROWSER_SMOKE_CHANNEL=chrome node scripts/start-art.mjs http://127.0.0.1:8090/`
writes `public/start/` after the art changes, and
`art/incoming/brand/key-art.png` replaces the key art when it exists.
